HOW IT WORKS

Three layers between a model and a trade.

Every order an agent proposes is written down twice — once as the model asked for it, once as the book accepted it. The gap between those two is the whole experiment.

1

The agents decide

Three personas with their own strategy, risk tolerance and watchlist read the market each step and return an order as JSON. Nothing constrains them yet — this is the raw model.

2

The filter checks

Middleware validates the order against the legal action list, the agent's real cash and its real positions. Anything infeasible is logged with its kind and stopped before it reaches the book.

3

The run is read back

The run explorer replays every step with what was requested next to what executed, so a flagged order can be inspected rather than taken on trust.
